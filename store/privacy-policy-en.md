# Privacy Policy — Currency Converter

_Effective date: 2026-05-28_

The "Currency Converter" app (the "App") respects your privacy. This document
explains what data is processed.

## Data we collect
The App **does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data**.
- No sign-up, no accounts.
- No ads, no third-party trackers or analytics.
- No access to contacts, location, files, camera, etc.

## On-device data
Your settings (rate source, base currency, favorites, notification options) and
the rate cache are stored **only locally** on your device and never leave it.
They are removed when you uninstall the App.

## Network requests
To fetch rates, the App contacts public sources:
- Central Bank of Russia — `https://www.cbr-xml-daily.ru`
- Open Exchange Rates (market rates) — `https://open.er-api.com`

These requests contain no personal data and are used only to download current
exchange rates.

## Notifications
The App may show local notifications (a daily rate summary). They are generated
on the device and are not sent anywhere.

## Children
The App is not directed at children and does not collect their data.
